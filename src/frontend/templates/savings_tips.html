Savings tips

  • Pay yourself first: move money to savings on payday.
  • Set a monthly budget and review it each week.
  • Build an emergency fund covering three months of expenses.
  • Cancel subscriptions you no longer use.
  • Wait 24 hours before any non-essential purchase.
  • Review your subscriptions every month and cancel the ones you don't use.
